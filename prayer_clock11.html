💰 Charity Does Not Decrease Wealth

Abu Huraira reported Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) as saying:

مَا نَقَصَتْ صَدَقَةٌ مِنْ مَالٍ وَمَا زَادَ اللَّهُ عَبْدًا بِعَفْوٍ إِلاَّ عِزًّا وَمَا تَوَاضَعَ أَحَدٌ لِلَّهِ إِلاَّ رَفَعَهُ اللَّهُ

  • Charity does not decrease wealth.
  • No one forgives another except that Allah increases his honor.
  • And no one humbles himself for the sake of Allah except that Allah raises his status.

📖 Sahih Muslim, 2588

🔥 Charity Extinguishes Sins

Ka'b bin Ujrah narrated, The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said to me:

‏‏‏‏‏‏يَا كَعْبَ بْنَ عُجْرَةَ الصَّلَاةُ بُرْهَانٌ وَالصَّوْمُ جُنَّةٌ حَصِينَةٌ، ‏‏‏‏‏‏وَالصَّدَقَةُ تُطْفِئُ الْخَطِيئَةَ كَمَا يُطْفِئُ الْمَاءُ النَّارَ،

  • Ka'ab bin Ujrah! Salat is clear proof,
  • And Sawm (fasting) is an impregnable shield,
  • And Sadaqah (charity) extinguishes sins just as water extinguishes fire.

📖 Jami` at-Tirmidhi 614 (Hasan Sahih)

☂️ Shade on the Day of Judgment

Uqbah b. ʿĀmir, I heard the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) say:

كُلُّ امْرِئٍ فِي ظِلِّ صَدَقَتِهِ حَتَّى يُفْصَلَ بَيْنَ النَّاسِ أَوْ قَالَ يُحْكَمَ بَيْنَ النَّاسِ قَالَ يَزِيدُ وَكَانَ أَبُو الْخَيْرِ لَا يُخْطِئُهُ يَوْمٌ إِلَّا تَصَدَّقَ فِيهِ بِشَيْءٍ وَلَوْ كَعْكَةً أَوْ بَصَلَةً أَوْ كَذَا

  • Every person will be in the shade of their charity until the judgment is passed between people.
  • " He said: "Abu Al-Khair would not let a day pass without giving charity,
  • Even if it was a small piece of bread or an onion or something similar.

📖 Musnad Ahmad 17333

🤫 Secret Charity = Great Reward

Narrated Abu Huraira: The Prophet (ﷺ) said:

‏‏‏‏‏‏يَا كَعْبَ بْنَ عُجْرَةَ الصَّلَاةُ بُرْهَانٌ وَالصَّوْمُ جُنَّةٌ حَصِينَةٌ، ‏‏‏‏‏‏وَالصَّدَقَةُ تُطْفِئُ الْخَطِيئَةَ كَمَا يُطْفِئُ الْمَاءُ النَّارَ،

  • Seven people will be shaded by Allah under His shade on the day when there will be no shade except His.
  • And one of them who who practices in charity
  • So secretly that his left hand does not know what his right hand has given (i.e. nobody knows how much he has given in charity).

📖 Sahih al-Bukhari 1423

♾️ Reward Continues After Death

Narrated Abu Huraira, The Prophet ﷺ said:

قَالَ ‏ "‏ إِذَا مَاتَ الإِنْسَانُ انْقَطَعَ عَنْهُ عَمَلُهُ إِلاَّ مِنْ ثَلاَثَةٍ إِلاَّ مِنْ صَدَقَةٍ جَارِيَةٍ أَوْ عِلْمٍ يُنْتَفَعُ بِهِ أَوْ وَلَدٍ صَالِحٍ يَدْعُو لَهُ ‏"‏

  • When a man dies, his acts come to an end, but three
  • Recurring charity, or knowledge (by which people) benefit
  • Or a pious child, who prays for him (for the deceased).

📖 Sahih Muslim 1631

🛡️ Protection from Hellfire

Narrated `Adi bin Hatim heard the Prophet (ﷺ) saying:

‏‏‏‏‏‏قَالَ سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ ‏ "‏ اتَّقُوا النَّارَ وَلَوْ بِشِقِّ تَمْرَةٍ ‏"‏‏.‏

  • "Save yourself from Hell-fire even by giving half a date-fruit in charity."

📖 Sahih al-Bukhari 1417, Sahih Muslim 1016

⛰️ Small Charity Becomes Huge Reward

Narrated Abu Huraira, The Prophet ﷺ said:

‏‏‏‏‏‏مَنْ تَصَدَّقَ بِعَدْلِ تَمْرَةٍ مِنْ كَسْبٍ طَيِّبٍ ـ وَلاَ يَقْبَلُ اللَّهُ إِلاَّ الطَّيِّبَ ـ وَإِنَّ اللَّهَ يَتَقَبَّلُهَا بِيَمِينِهِ، ثُمَّ يُرَبِّيهَا لِصَاحِبِهِ كَمَا يُرَبِّي أَحَدُكُمْ فَلُوَّهُ حَتَّى تَكُونَ مِثْلَ الْجَبَلِ

  • If one give in charity what equals one date-fruit from the honestly earned money.
  • Allah takes it in His right (hand) and then enlarges its reward for that person.
  • Allah nurtures it until it becomes like a mountain.

📖 Sahih al-Bukhari 1410, Sahih Muslim 1014
